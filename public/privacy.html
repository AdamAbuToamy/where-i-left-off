Privacy Policy

This policy covers Where I Left Off, published by Adam Abu Toamy. Updated October 18, 2026.

Your account and content

Google Firebase provides hosting, authentication and cloud storage. We process your account ID, email, display name, and the cards, links, checkpoints, notes, checklists, sections, activity sessions and preferences you choose to save. Authentication providers handle sign-in credentials.

Visibility and sharing

Personal workspace content is restricted to your account. Group members can access shared group content according to their roles. Share links and invitations only with intended recipients. Administrators can access account management records, support requests and diagnostic records; operational access to the backend is restricted to authorized operators.

Support and diagnostics

The private support form stores your account ID, request type, subject, message, language, timestamps, status and administrator reply. Send only information necessary for your request. The service also records account email and first/last activity for administration, and limited technical diagnostics: account ID, event, error code, time, app version, environment, route without private parameters, duration and sanitized source location. Diagnostic events do not intentionally contain card contents, passwords or full captured URLs. Infrastructure providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses.

Storage on your device

IndexedDB and local storage keep offline copies, pending edits, session history, preferences, installation reminders and a bounded diagnostic buffer. Signing out separates accounts but may retain offline content on that device. On shared devices, use a separate browser profile and clear site data when finished; clearing it can discard unsynchronized edits.

External services and the extension

Optional book searches use Open Library; TV searches use TVMaze. Searches and cover-image requests disclose request information, including search terms where applicable and your network address, to the relevant provider. External links open their own services. The Chrome extension sends the selected page title, URL and available video position to the site after you click it. Capture parameters may appear in browser history. The site can ask a configured extension to confirm installation; that check does not read other browsing data. See the separate extension privacy policy.

Purpose and retention

We use information to provide sync, offline access, collaboration, account protection and support. We do not use your saved content for advertising or sell it. Account content remains until you remove it or complete account deletion; moving a card to trash is not permanent deletion. Support requests remain available for follow-up until removed with account deletion or by the operator. Diagnostic buffers are limited to 200 events on this device; server retention depends on administrative cleanup. Do not treat the service as your only backup.

Your choices and account deletion

Settings provides a personal JSON export of cards, sections, workflow preferences and activity sessions; group records and server diagnostics are excluded. You can edit or remove saved content. Automatic account deletion requires recent sign-in and an available deletion worker. A confirmed request can be restored within 24 hours; afterward the next successful worker run removes the account and associated personal data. Shared group content may remain for other members, with membership removed and ownership transferred where needed. Provider backups or security/legal records may remain under applicable retention requirements. If automatic deletion is unavailable, send a private account-deletion request in Support; this is a manual request and does not itself pause or delete your account.

Contact and changes

Use the private support form for access, correction, export, deletion or privacy questions. Sign in to verify which account your request concerns. No password or access token is needed in your message. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
