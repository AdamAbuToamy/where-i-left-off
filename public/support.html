Support and feedback

Private support for Where I Left Off.

Send a request

Open the app and sign in, then choose Support and feedback in the footer or Settings. Select a suggestion, bug, privacy request or account deletion. Describe the issue without passwords, access tokens or private third-party information. Requests and replies are visible to you and the administrator. There is no guaranteed response time.

Account deletion

Settings offers automatic deletion when the worker is available. If unavailable, choose Account deletion in the support form. A manual request is reviewed by the operator; sending it does not delete your account immediately.

Access trouble

Try your sign-in provider or the password reset on the sign-in screen. The private form requires sign-in to verify account ownership. Do not post private account details publicly.
